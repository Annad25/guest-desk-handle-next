# Final app task submission

## User and plan

Who is this for, what does success look like, and what did you choose to build? Include your initial plan and assumptions.

## Implementation

Paste a focused diff or a code link the reviewer can access. Explain how the UI uses the new backend logic and what your small usability improvement does for the operator.

## Working with AI

Include the AI tool used and a few decisive prompts or conversation excerpts. Show at least one result you checked, corrected, or chose not to use, if that happened. Explain the decision; do not invent an iteration to fill the template.

## Verification

List commands or browser actions, their observed results, and remaining gaps. Separate checks you ran from checks you only planned. Include the new endpoint, tie handling, resolved requests, empty queue, completion flow, and errors.

## Handoff

What works, what remains, and what would you improve next?

## Optional reflection (unscored)

What did you enjoy or find frustrating about the work?
